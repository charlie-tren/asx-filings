10 September 2026 ASX Announcement 
 
Chief Financial Officer Appointment & Trading Update 
 
 
DroneShield Limited (ASX:DRO) (DroneShield or the Company) announces: 
• the appointment of Rebecca Lowde as Chief Financial Officer (CFO); 
• FY2026 Committed Revenue has surpassed $250M; and 
• the first order of RfRecon, the Company’s recently released AI-enabled device. 
 
Appointment of CFO 
Rebecca Lowde will be joining DroneShield as CFO on 2 November 2026. Rebecca is a public-
company and technology-sector finance executive with three decades of e xperience across 
CFO, CEO and enterprise leadership roles. She brings expertise in transformation, capital 
management, M&A, governance, risk and scaling global technology-enabled businesses. 
 
Most recently, Rebecca served as CFO at MYOB, the KKR -owned accounting and business 
management software provider, where she oversaw a debt refinancing and EBITDA growth 
through product monetisation and expansion, embedded financial services and disciplined 
cost management. 
 
Previously, Rebecca was CFO and Chief People Officer at Afterpay, where she supported a 
$1.5B capital raising and the company’s $39 B acquisition by Block. She has also served as 
CFO and CEO of ASX -listed Salmat, and Executive Director and CFO of Bravura Solutions . 
Rebecca is a CPA, a Graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors and a member 
of Chief Executive Women. 
 
After more than eight years with DroneShield, current CFO and Joint Company Secretary 
Carla Balanco has decided to leave the Company. The Board thanks Carla for her contribution 
and commitment over the years . Following Rebecca’s arrival, Carla will provide support as 
needed with transition arrangements. 
 
Trading Update 
Since the release of 1H 2026 Results, FY2026 Committed Revenue1 has grown to $251M and 
now within the FY2026 revenue outlook range of $250M to $270M . This milestone reflects 
continued conversion of global demand into contracted revenue. Committed Revenue for 
FY2027 and beyond is currently at $46M. 
 
DroneShield has also accepted the first order for its recently released, AI-enabled device, 
RfRecon, which will see hardware deployed to an existing Western European military end-
user by the end of 2026. While not a material contract, it is an important validation of the 
Company’s next-generation counter-drone solutions. 
 
 
1 Committed Revenue is the value of hardware, services, warranties and subscriptions delivered and scheduled for delivery within the 
period, based on confirmed purchase orders and prevailing exchange rates when reported. FY2026 Committed Revenue was last 
reported at $240M as at 21 August 2026, and now reported at $251M as at 8 September 2026.

About DroneShield Limited 
DroneShield (ASX:DRO) is a global leader in AI -powered counter -drone and uncrewed, 
autonomous systems defence. We develop and deploy interoperable sensing, electronic warfare 
and command-and-control solutions that detect, track, identify and defeat drone threats across 
fixed-site, dismounted and on -the-move operations. Trusted by military, government, law 
enforcement, critical infrastructure and com mercial customers and active  globally, DroneShield 
combines operationally proven technology with continuous R&D and AI-software driven innovation. 
We master complexity to protect the people, places and assets that matter.
