• Sale remains a key strategic and management priority as part of Perpetual’s ongoing simplification
Use of proceeds
• Net proceeds2 will be used to repay debt (Facility D – Bridge)
• Subject to completion and current assumptions regarding consideration, transaction and separation costs, tax and other completion adjustments, 
Perpetual expects to be in a net cash position on a pro-forma basis following completion
